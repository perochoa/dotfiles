# AI assistant corrections log

Behavioural corrections I've given AI coding assistants, so repeats can be spotted and turned into
rules or hooks. Machine-local (`ai/corrections.local.md`, created from `ai/corrections.template.md`).
Assistants append entries; see "Learning from corrections" in `ai/AGENTS.md`.

Format, one line per correction, newest last:
`- YYYY-MM-DD | <project or global> | <correction, one line> | <status>`

Status is one of: `seen` (first time), `codified: <where>` (now a rule/hook),
`declined` (I said not to codify it; don't ask again), `merged into <date>` (duplicate).
